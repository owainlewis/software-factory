# Pipeline CRM

A small CRM for a sales team of five to twenty people.

## What it does

- Keep track of **contacts** and the **companies** they work for.
- Track **deals** through a sales pipeline, from lead to won or lost.
- Log **activities** (notes, calls, emails, meetings) against contacts, companies and deals, and see them on a timeline.
- Find anything fast with **search**.
- A simple **web interface** for the team, and a **JSON API** for integrations.
- **Accounts** so each salesperson signs in and owns their deals.
- **CSV import and export**, and a **dashboard** of the numbers that matter.
